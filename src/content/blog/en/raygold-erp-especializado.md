## What is RayGold?

RayGold is a [jewelry ERP software](/en) designed specifically for the industry. Unlike generic ERPs such as SAP Business One, Odoo or Microsoft Dynamics 365, RayGold understands the particularities of the sector: precious metal traceability, scrap control in casting, gold ledger management and compliance with specific regulations such as VERI*FACTU.

## Main modules

### Master Files

The master files module manages all base information: items with material composition, suppliers, customers and product families. Each item can define its bill of materials (BOM) with alloys, precious stones and components.

### Manufacturing

The heart of RayGold for workshops and factories. Work order management, scrap control at each phase of the production process, integration with 3D printing for prototypes and molds, and complete traceability from raw material to finished product.

### Sales

Sales, order and invoicing management adapted to the sector. Pricing by metal weight, material deposit management, and digital catalog for sales representatives.

## Why choose RayGold?

The main advantage of a [jewelry ERP](/en) like RayGold over generic ERPs is that you don't need customization. The specific functionalities of the jewelry sector come integrated as standard, which reduces implementation cost and start-up time.
